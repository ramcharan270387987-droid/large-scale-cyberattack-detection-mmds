# Scientific support and limitations

All historical source data have been repeatedly examined. Primary E is a retrospective earlier-start study with seven forward capture arrivals. The original chronological B test has two capture days. Neither is an untouched independent test. Flow counts are not independent experimental replications, and family and capture day are strongly confounded. Permanent group-role assignment prevents fitting/evaluation leakage while preserving within-role recurrence and multiplicity.

Historical KSWIN needs100 ordered monitoring observations. ADWIN uses frozen clock32 and grace10; seven capture arrivals cannot support a meaningful advanced-trigger efficacy comparison. No genuine intraday ordered binary error sequence is available for DDM. These states are unsupported, not no-drift observations. Frozen JS .1 uses a model-independent initial-fit input reference; its historical alerts have no authoritative concept-drift labels. No outcome-driven threshold reduction is permitted to manufacture opportunities.

Guard efficacy on real traffic is unsupported if the frozen trigger produces no eligible historical fitting requests. Zero requests and zero natural rollbacks remain real observations, with engineering behavior separately verified through synthetic and injected tests. A family with no post-exposure forward evaluation cannot establish learning after exposure. Retention uses separate earlier calibration observations and is a retrospective forgetting diagnostic, not independent generalization.

Synthetic mechanisms supply controlled causal changes and three deterministic seeds. Their local sklearn LR differs from historical Spark LR and cannot establish operational cybersecurity effectiveness. Input JS can miss conditional reversals. A guarded policy can retain older performance at the cost of rejecting a useful changed-concept candidate. Prespecified negative results remain in all tables.

The operating ceiling is1% benign FPR on currently eligible promotion-validation rows. Future realized FPR may exceed it. Score-bin areas are approximate at2048 bins, with exact tie-aware integer confusion matrices at declared bin thresholds. Pooled multi-version scores may not share calibration. Undefined metrics remain blank. All-issued eventual-label outcomes and released-by-termination diagnostics are explicitly separate.

Costs are measured wall time at the reported granularity, not CPU consumption or production latency. Unique physical builds and counterfactual charged policy costs must not be summed together. Private scratch row predictions disappear with the Kaggle runtime; durable compact bin/family/version counts reproduce configured decisions. Saved full models and their hashes remain private outputs. No multi-node scalability claim or live autonomous-retraining deployment is made.
