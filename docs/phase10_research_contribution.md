# Phase 10 research contribution

This phase supplies reproducible frequent-pattern and temporal support evidence for a previously examined network-flow corpus. Standard Spark FP-Growth, count-derived association metrics and deterministic discretization provide the implementation. The contribution lies in the traceable experiment, exact class- and capture-conditioned support, duplicate-weighting comparisons, and validation-constrained chronological pattern assessment.

The negative operational result is central. A ranking score can achieve measurable ROC discrimination while a validation-constrained policy produces no alerts under later capture conditions. The five-bin sensitivity detects few attacks and generates substantially more false alarms than true positives. Frequent retrospective associations consequently require separate operational evaluation.

Capture-conditioned pattern changes are exploratory descriptions. The study cannot identify causal mechanisms, establish concept drift from frequency alone, or infer discovery of new cyberattacks. Independent external validation and broader scalability are reserved for Phase 14.
