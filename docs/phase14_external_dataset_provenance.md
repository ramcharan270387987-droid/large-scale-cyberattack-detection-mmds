This claim is retained verbatim in the metadata receipt; it is not independent proof of byte identity to an official archive. It uses a testbed with profiled benign users and scheduled attacks, not independent production traffic. The eight filenames and labels will be checked during ingestion. Official dataset page: https://www.unb.ca/cic/datasets/ids-2017.html . Original paper: Sharafaldin, Lashkari and Ghorbani, ICISSP2018, “Toward Generating a New Intrusion Detection Dataset and Intrusion Traffic Characterization”. Cite this source and preserve original labels.

The mirror declaration is not represented as a relicensing guarantee from UNB. Research use here retains attribution and is noncommercial.

Neither release's exact extraction build is authenticated. Semantic transfer therefore uses only duration and two packet counts under the declared conditional compatibility contract. No claim of bit-equivalent feature extraction or exact18-feature archived transfer is made. Raw CSV lacks event timestamps/addresses; capture dates derive from the eight day filenames, and exact cross-dataset event identity cannot be established from this anonymized release.
