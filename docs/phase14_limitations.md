# Phase14 scientific limitations

The source is the exact16,232,943-flow MMDS Kaggle release,whose upstream description and extraction revision are not authenticated. It is not claimed to be the complete officialCSE-CIC2018corpus. The externalCICIDS2017mirror has explicitUNBprovenance and pinned observed file hashes,but no independently authenticated official-archive digest chain. Both are historical laboratory datasets. Distinct collection dates and schemas support an external comparison;they do not establish production effectiveness.

Only three duration/count features have a defensible conditional common contract. Exact18-feature archived external transfer remains unsupported. No missing required predictor is replaced by an invented zero. Empirical duration/rate consistency supports the documented microsecond convention;it cannot prove identical extractor builds,timeouts or packet-level measurement. Other packet-length,rate,ratio andTCPflag semantics remain unverified across revisions. No direct treeSHAP or causal attribution is fabricated.

Within-target common-vector roles are disjoint and chronological comparisons purge later-role collisions. Common3is coarse and can purge substantial later-capture coverage. Original flow multiplicities and conflicting labels remain. Feature collisions across datasets are not proof that two flows are the same network event. The collision-exclusion sensitivity is conservative and changes the evaluated population. Source/external differences can reflect measurement,prevalence,family,capture and selection differences. Ranking performance and frozen operating-point performance are distinct. A1%validation benignFPR ceiling is not a futureFPR guarantee.

Group-aware training thresholds do not guarantee the same fraction of flows. In the secondary grouped target comparison,the training-role hash and sampling hash are shared:the global1000/10000threshold applies within a7000/10000training-role prefix,resulting in approximately1/7of eligible training groups rather than an independent10%within-role sample. This is a disclosed secondary sampling-budget deviation;actual groups,flows and fractions are independently reported. Primary capture-chronological training is unaffected. Neither comparison is described as exactly matched training effort.

Representative error diagnostics use distinct predictor-group representatives. Enriched cases use at most8per external family/outcome and do not estimate prevalence. Case values from strata<5are suppressed in public aggregates. AdditiveLRcontributions are log-odds,not additive probabilities. The mirror's literalU+FFFDcharacters in three WebAttack labels are preserved with exact byte catalogs;intended punctuation is unknown and not repaired.

Computational benchmarks use actual observed rows and fixed materializing actions. local[1]/[2]/[4]are single-machine configurations,not nodes. NewSparkContexts can share a warmedJVM,and operating-system cache is uncontrolled. Data-volume and worker comparisons have matched logical inputs and retained repetitions,but sharedKagglehardware can vary. SampledRSSpeaks can miss transients;configured heap is notRSS. Unretrieved shuffle/spill/eviction metrics remain unsupported rather than invented. Wall time is notCPUconsumption. BoundedPandas comparisons do not establish universalSpark superiority. One budgeted100kFP-Growth fit is cost evidence,not a stable scaling estimate. Earlier streaming replay evidence is reused and clearly identified.

External scientific validity,computational scaling,engineering correctness and production readiness are separate conclusions. No Phase15/16implementation or public deployment is included.

Pandas and Spark actions share one notebook process tree. Process-tree/JVM RSS during a Pandas action can include the resident Spark JVM;these values are environment peaks,not isolated Pandas allocation or incremental engine memory. Python/JVM peaks are retained separately. Pandas import/startup was not isolated independently;SparkContext initialization and operation boundaries are reported separately. Input-loading comparisons force the same checksum rather than measuring an unused lazy read.
