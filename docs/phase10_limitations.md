# Phase 10 scientific limitations

The observed corpus was examined in previous phases. External validation is reserved for Phase 14. Families and capture days are strongly confounded. Frequency, lift and prevalence change cannot establish causal attack mechanisms or concept drift. Newly observed patterns may reflect traffic composition, capture conditions, or threshold crossings. Within-class comparisons require actual support for both relevant classes.

Mining uses six registered numerical features while transaction auditing covers all 18 predictors. Full-population execution therefore describes the observation count and the six-feature search space. Three- and five-bin quantiles may collapse because of zero inflation or ties. Exported itemsets and rules are bounded to 500 rows with full cardinalities recorded separately. Filtering exported lengths does not constrain the FP-Growth search.

Spark execution uses a single private CPU node. Recorded timings include their specified computation scopes and do not demonstrate multi-node performance or linear scalability. Correlated flows and ten captures do not provide independent flow-level replications. Descriptive capture-day and predictor-group sensitivity is preferred to unsupported confidence intervals.

PrefixSpan requires defensible session identifiers, ordering and event semantics. The primary feature-ready contract omits IP addresses and session identifiers from predictors, and a record fingerprint establishes identity rather than temporal sequence. Sequence support must be established from verified metadata before any sequential claim is made. Sequential mining remains unexecuted.
