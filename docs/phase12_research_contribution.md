# Phase 12 research contribution

The contribution is an auditable predict-before-update comparison with simulated label release, immutable saved-model versions, explicit operating-point guards, matched historical cohorts, controlled synthetic mechanisms and counterfactual cost accounting. Results include negative refresh tradeoffs and unsupported temporal claims. Source receipts, prediction histograms and independent reloads permit reproduction. This study does not establish operational cyber-defense effectiveness or universally beneficial adaptation.
