# Phase 8 prespecified research protocol

Version `phase8-v1` tests whether a validation-constrained hybrid improves detection under shifted traffic. H1 concerns threshold selection, H2 class weighting, H3 incremental value from novelty fusion, and H4 selective review. Higher recall with a higher realized false-positive burden is a tradeoff. A negative or unsupported result is retained.

## Evaluation populations

B and C reconstruct the retained Phase 4/5 predictor-group benchmark populations and reload their exact models. Both are retrospective. B uses reference days through February 22, validation on February 23 and 28, and March 1–2 evaluation. C holds out February 22–23 and uses February 28 validation. C is a capture scenario with later reference days. The validation predictor groups are assigned by a fixed hash to three disjoint uses for probability calibration, meta-model fitting and operating selection. No original benchmark rows are reassigned across train, validation and test.

The rolling outer folds are independently specified in the configuration. R1 evaluates February 21 and R2 evaluates February 28. Earlier dates are assigned in the order fit, probability calibration and operating selection. A deterministic predictor-group hash divides the probability date into probability and learned-fusion partitions. A role with fewer than 100 rows of either class after purging is unsupported. Even the rolling folds use previously studied data. The dataset has ten observed capture days and strong family/day dependence. Independent external confirmation is deferred.

The 18-feature numerical registry stays frozen. No labels, capture days, timestamps, IPs, ports, identifiers or fingerprints enter predictions. Predictor, canonical-feature and exact-content fingerprints are audit keys. Retain the earliest role for repeated predictor groups. Measure imputed-vector overlap separately and retain the original benchmark cohorts for reconciliation. Retrospective calibration is group-separated and uses no test rows. For all comparisons within a fold, use the same retained test population.

## Frozen choices

Use fixed 2048-bin score histograms, inclusive lower bin edges and conservative whole tied bins. A no-positive threshold is always feasible. Primary selection maximizes operating-split recall at a 1% benign FPR ceiling, with precision, lower FPR, measured fit cost and simplicity as tie breakers. Also measure 0.1%, 0.5%, 2% and 5% operating ceilings, fixed 0.5, F1, F2, balanced accuracy and a five-to-one false-negative cost. Report realized later FPR at unchanged thresholds. PR-AUC is the binned average precision approximation, with complete ROC and PR support obtained from aggregate buckets.

Fit preprocessing on training predictor groups only. Train weight-enabled Spark classifiers on the same deterministic 6.25% sample where practical. The prespecified weight candidates are 1, 2, 5, 10 and the ratio of training benign to training attacks. LR and RF are primary, and weighted GBT is a bounded secondary comparison. Fit Platt-style and isotonic calibration on disjoint probability data; report Brier, log loss and ten equal-width reliability bins with 1e-6 log clipping. Use reference-only K-Means with strict baseline P99 exceedance. Empirical novelty normalization uses a bounded sorted reference quantile grid, with strict-less rank for ties. All score fusion rates and percentiles are reference-relative scores.

Prespecify supervised and novelty alone, OR and AND breach of validation-budget component thresholds, weighted fusion over alphas 0, .25, .5, .75 and 1, and regularized learned logistic fusion on disjoint scored groups where supported. OR and AND use the equivalent max/min of the component threshold margins and are evaluated at their literal decisions, with combined FPR eligibility checked separately. Freeze fitted parameters, thresholds and validation selection before aggregate evaluation. The triage high threshold uses 1% FPR selection and the lower threshold uses 5%. Between them is review, below both is low risk, and unsupported inputs abstain. Compare automatic coverage and error, review burden, and false alarms on all rows.

Use four Spark workers on Kaggle CPU with an 8 GiB driver and bounded local model samples. Persist compact Parquet projections and aggregate histograms only. Report capture-day sensitivity and paired same-population differences. The small number of days precludes strong uncertainty claims from row-level independence. Do not stop early based on favorable outcomes.
